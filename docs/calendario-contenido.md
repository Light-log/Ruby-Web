## Semana 1 — textos listos

**LinkedIn (martes, inglés):**
> Rails, Node.js or Django for your next business app? 🧭
>
> We get this question in almost every discovery call. The honest answer: the framework matters less
> than who will maintain it — but the trade-offs are real:
>
> • Rails: fastest path to a solid CRUD-heavy app, admin panels and APIs; smaller talent pool.
> • Node.js: one language front and back, huge ecosystem; more decisions to make yourself.
> • Django: great when the product leans on data or Python ML; similar productivity to Rails.
>
> We compared them for real business cases (internal tools, SaaS, APIs) 👇
> https://devruby.org/us/blog/ruby-on-rails-vs-node-vs-django
>
> #RubyOnRails #SoftwareDevelopment #SaaS
