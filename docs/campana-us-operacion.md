# U.S. campaign operation

## Published routes

- `/us`
- `/us/custom-internal-tools`
- `/us/workflow-automation`
- `/us/api-integration-services`
- `/us/application-security-audit`

The campaign is English-language, original content for U.S. businesses. It is not an IP redirect and it does not claim a local office. The public position is: **DEVRUBY LLC is a U.S. company working remotely nationwide.**

## Lead attribution

- Booking links attach `utm_source=devruby.org`, `utm_medium=organic`, and `utm_campaign=us`.
- Campaign form links use `?origen=us`.
- Emails received through the form include `Origen: us` and have the subject prefix `[U.S.]`.

## First acquisition cycle

1. Submit the U.S. routes in Search Console and request indexing after deployment.
2. Run one Google Search campaign only after checking GA4 and form delivery.
3. Keep one ad group for each intent: internal tools, workflow automation, and API integration.
4. Exclude job seekers, courses, templates, free software, tutorials, and open-source research traffic.
5. Record each lead as `us` and qualify it by company, workflow, systems, decision-maker, timeline, and budget fit.

## Evidence used on the campaign

- Lazo is described as a mobile application published in both Apple App Store and Google Play, as authorized by the owner.
- Maintenance Check is described by its delivered technical scope only.
- No quantified outcome, client logo, testimonial, compliance claim, or named customer is added without clear authorization.

## Before increasing ad spend

Confirm all of the following:

1. A form test reaches the destination inbox and can be replied to.
2. A Calendly test booking is available in U.S. time zones.
3. GA4 records booking, WhatsApp, and successful form events only after consent.
4. At least one conversion is manually checked against the email/Calendly record.
5. A daily lead-response owner is assigned; reply within one business day.
